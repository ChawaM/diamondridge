Founded

Diamond Ridge Engineering Limited was established on 26th May, 2020, responding to a growing demand for Civil, Mechanical, and Electrical Engineering solutions in Zambia. In just five years, we have grown into a full-service engineering company recognised across multiple industries.

Operating on a team concept, our skilled and experienced staff and field teams have established a standard of excellence well recognised in the construction industry. We bring competitive pricing, diverse capabilities, and a proven track record to every project.

From service station construction and office fit-outs, to medical oxygen plants and large-scale infrastructure — we deliver on time and within budget, guided by strong executive leadership and sound ethical principles.
